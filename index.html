What can I use this for?

Why did their division beat mine?

Build both templates in Division Lab and check the matchup directly. Armor, piercing, hardness, attack, defense or breakthrough, organization, width and terrain are all in the same place. Division Lab guide.

What should I change?

Counter Analysis tries focused changes against the exact enemy division you entered. It shows which changes move the modeled matchup and how the recommended template changes the per-division IC cost. Counter Analysis guide.

Is this tank or plane actually better?

Build the variants, compare the stats, then use the finished equipment in the scenario it is meant for. Tank Designer and Air Lab cover the equipment side.

Is my division good outside one matchup?

Run Gauntlet. Full Gauntlet throws the division at 10,000 deterministic opponents across eight terrain types while attacking and defending. Gauntlet guide.
