DIVISION GAUNTLET · HOI4 1.19.3

Find out what your division hates fighting

A template can crush the opponent you built it for and still have a huge blind spot. Gauntlet changes the opponents and terrain so those problems have a chance to show up.

Quick or full?

Quick Gauntlet samples roughly 500 opponent designs when you want a fast check. Full Gauntlet uses 10,000 deterministic opponents across eight terrain types while attacking and defending, which produces 160,000 matchup checks for the selected division.

What to look for

Do not just look at the top-line grade. Check what types of opponents cause trouble. A division that looks great against soft infantry may fall apart against armor, lose too much while attacking, or give up most of its advantage in bad terrain. Those patterns are the useful part of the run.

Run A/B tests

The opponent set is deterministic, so you can run a baseline, change the division, and run the same broad test again. That makes Gauntlet useful after a Counter recommendation: you can see whether fixing one matchup made the division worse somewhere else.

It is not a universal tier list

Gauntlet grades and rankings are planner analytical. They do not know what your next opponent will build, what your industry looks like, or which terrain matters most in your campaign. Use the broad result to find questions, then go back to Division Lab for the fights that actually matter.

Accuracy boundary

Gauntlet reuses the planner's current data and combat model, then adds deterministic opponent generation and scoring on top. See the methodology page for the current evidence boundary.
