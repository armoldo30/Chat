Send it into Division Lab

The useful question is usually not “which tank has the biggest number?” It is whether the variant changes the division enough to matter. Test whether extra armor crosses a threshold, whether more attack changes the exchange, or whether an expensive module barely moves the matchup.

Change one big thing at a time

Duplicate a baseline and swap one gun, armor package or major upgrade. That makes the tradeoff much easier to read than changing half the tank at once.
