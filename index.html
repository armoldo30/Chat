Understand why a division won, test the counter, and improve the next design.

HOI4 War Planner is an independent analytical companion for Hearts of Iron IV. It combines a bundled vanilla 1.19.2 data baseline with Division Lab, Counter Analysis, Tank Designer, Air Lab and Division Gauntlet. The site is built for theorycrafting and post-game review: recreate a matchup, identify the interaction that mattered, test changes, and compare the combat gain against the equipment cost.

PLAYER GUIDE

What the planner is designed to answer

Why did this division beat mine?

Recreate both templates in Division Lab and compare the interaction directly. Armor and piercing thresholds, hardness, attack profile, defense or breakthrough, organization, width and terrain can be inspected together instead of guessing from one headline stat. Division Lab guide.

What is the best counter?

Counter Analysis tests focused structural changes against the exact target division and ranks the useful ones by modeled matchup improvement. The goal is to explain what changes the result, not to declare one universal template. Counter Analysis guide.

How should I design the equipment?

Use the designers to compare equipment tradeoffs, then test the finished variant in the wider scenario. Tank Designer and Air Lab keep the equipment question connected to the matchup it is meant to solve.

Is the design broadly good?

Division Gauntlet tests a design across large deterministic opponent sets and terrain conditions. Use it when the question is general robustness rather than one specific multiplayer matchup. Gauntlet guide.

Evidence policy: game-file exact values are kept distinct from executable-inferred behavior and planner-analytical scoring.
