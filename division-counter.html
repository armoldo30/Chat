DIVISION LAB · COUNTER ANALYSIS · HOI4 1.19.3

Build the fight you actually want to solve

Put your division on one side, the enemy on the other, and test the matchup directly. Counter then tries changes against that enemy instead of handing you a generic “best template.”

Start with the matchup

Build both templates and set the battlefield assumptions you care about. Division Lab lets you look at armor and piercing, hardness, soft and hard attack, defense or breakthrough, organization, width, terrain and equipment together. That is usually more useful than staring at one headline stat.

What Counter actually searches

The normal search tests bounded one- and two-change candidates from the current battalion, divisional-support, Regimental Support and active tank-design options. Candidates are screened for the selected enemy and the final recommendations are battle-simulated. If normal search finds no meaningful answer, an optional bounded third change can be tried when a +2 percentage-point improvement is still possible.

How recommendations are different

Best Raw is the strongest combat result. Best Efficient balances improvement against direct IC per division, modeled supply use and change complexity. Smallest Effective Change looks for the least disruptive change that still clears the meaningful-improvement threshold. Duplicate headline winners are collapsed so you get distinct alternatives instead of the same answer three times.

What the cost number means

Counter shows the current division equipment cost and the recommended cost as X IC/div → Y IC/div, plus the absolute and percentage change. It deliberately does not use your saved production lines, spare military factories, resource plan or retooling assumptions to suppress a battlefield recommendation. Whether you can afford the change in a live campaign is left to you.

Use the result, then widen the test

If a recommendation helps, look at why: piercing threshold, hardness-sensitive attack, breakthrough, defense, organization, width, supply or some combination of them. Once the specific matchup makes sense, run Division Gauntlet to see whether the new design created a different weakness somewhere else.

Accuracy boundary

Counter search and recommendation ranking are planner analytical. The combat model underneath them has its own evidence labels. The project does not claim bit-for-bit parity with hoi4.exe; see the methodology page for the current boundary.
