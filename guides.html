How to use HOI4 War Planner

Build or recreate the divisions you want to understand, compare the exact matchup in Division Lab, then use Counter Analysis and Gauntlet to test how you would improve it.

Recreate the matchup

Division Lab is designed for comparison, not for chasing one headline statistic. Build your division and the target division, then match the technology, doctrine, MIO, equipment and battlefield assumptions you want to test.

Diagnose and counter

Counter Analysis is for a specific opponent. It highlights interactions such as armor versus piercing and soft versus hard attack, then tests focused structural changes against that exact target. Recommendations include the combat change and the equipment-cost tradeoff rather than ranking strength alone.

Design equipment deliberately

Tank Designer and Air Lab feed variant assumptions into the analysis. For useful experiments, change one variable at a time and keep the comparison target stable so you can see what actually caused the result to move.

Use Gauntlet to find blind spots

Counter Analysis answers a specific matchup question; Division Gauntlet asks whether a division is broadly robust. Quick Gauntlet samples roughly 500 opponents. Full Gauntlet uses 10,000 deterministic opponent designs across eight terrain types while attacking and defending, for 160,000 matchups.

Read evidence labels

Game-file exact is retained directly from supplied 1.19.2 files. Executable inferred is constrained by evidence but not provable from data files alone. Planner analytical is an explicit comparison or scoring abstraction.

The project does not claim bit-for-bit parity with hoi4.exe.
