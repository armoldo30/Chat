How to use HOI4 War Planner

Start with the question you actually have. Build the matchup, test the change, and only add more assumptions when they matter.

Trying to explain a battle?

Open Division Lab and build your division and the enemy division. Set the terrain and the tech, doctrine, MIO and equipment assumptions you want to reproduce. You can then look at armor versus piercing, hardness, attack, defense or breakthrough, organization and width together instead of guessing from one stat.

Trying to find a counter?

Counter Analysis works against the exact opponent you built. It tests focused battalion, support and tank-design changes, then battle-simulates the candidates that make it through the bounded search. A useful result shows both the modeled combat gain and the change in equipment IC per division. It does not guess whether your current factory setup can afford the change.

Comparing tanks or aircraft?

Use the Tank Designer or Air Lab. A good way to test a design is to duplicate your baseline, change one important module or upgrade, and compare again. That makes it much easier to tell whether the extra cost actually bought something useful.

Not sure if the division is generally good?

Division Gauntlet checks a much wider set of opponents. Quick Gauntlet uses roughly 500 designs. Full Gauntlet uses 10,000 deterministic opponents across eight terrain types while attacking and defending, for 160,000 matchup checks. It is a stress test, not a universal tier list.

What do the accuracy labels mean?

The site does not treat every number as equally certain. game-file exact means the value or relationship comes directly from the retained 1.19.3 files. executable inferred means the behavior is evidence-based but not fully proven from data files alone. planner analytical means the planner created the score, search, comparison or scenario itself.

Oracle testing adds narrower executable evidence where we can control the experiment. The planner does not claim bit-for-bit parity with hoi4.exe. The methodology page has the full evidence policy.
