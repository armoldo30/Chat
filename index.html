PLAYER GUIDE

What do you want to test?

Why am I losing this matchup?

Build both divisions in Division Lab and set the terrain, doctrine, tech, equipment, and other assumptions you care about. It puts armor, piercing, hardness, attack, defense, breakthrough, organization, and width in one place so you can see what is actually driving the fight. Division Lab guide.

What should I change?

Counter Analysis tries focused changes against the exact enemy division you built. It battle-tests the candidates, shows the modeled improvement, and tells you how the new template changes IC per division. Counter Analysis guide.

What tank or plane should I build?

Use Tank Designer or Air Lab to compare variants without losing sight of the matchup they are meant to solve. Change a module, check the stats, then see whether the change actually matters.

Is this division good outside one fight?

Division Gauntlet is the broader check. Full Gauntlet runs the design against 10,000 deterministic opponents across eight terrains while attacking and defending.

Accuracy: the planner keeps game-file exact data separate from executable inferred behavior and planner analytical scoring. Oracle tests can narrow that boundary, but the project does not claim bit-for-bit parity with hoi4.exe. Read the methodology.
