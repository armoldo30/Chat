TANK DESIGNER · HOI4 1.19.3

Build the tank, then see if it actually helps

A tank can look great on the designer card and barely move the division matchup. Build the variant here, then judge it by what it does in the template you plan to field.

Compare the tradeoffs

Choose a chassis, role and compatible modules and check the resulting attack, armor, piercing, breakthrough, reliability, speed and production cost. Source-backed slot and compatibility rules keep the design inside the retained vanilla 1.19.3 design space.

Put it in a division

The useful question is rarely “which tank has the biggest number?” A variant matters because battalions use it. Send the design into Division Lab and check whether the extra armor crosses the enemy's piercing, whether the attack profile fits the target, or whether an expensive module barely changes the fight.

Watch the IC

Better stats are not free. Compare the IC cost alongside the combat result so you can decide whether the improvement is worth paying for. The planner gives you the design and division cost context; it does not pretend to know the exact factory plan of your live campaign.

A simple way to test modules

Keep one design as the baseline. Change one important module or upgrade on the copy and compare them again. If you change the gun, armor, engine and special modules all at once, you may get a better tank without learning which change did the work.

Accuracy boundary

Certified chassis, module and compatibility data come from the retained 1.19.3 source baseline. Combat interpretation has separate evidence labels. See the methodology page for the current boundary.
