ACCURACY POLICY

What is exact, what is inferred, and what is ours

HOI4 War Planner uses a bundled vanilla 1.19.3 baseline. Some values come straight from retained game files, some behavior has to be inferred, and some outputs are intentionally planner-created analysis.

Evidence classes

Game-file exact means the retained 1.19.3 files directly support the value or relationship. Executable inferred means the behavior is constrained by evidence but is not fully provable from those files alone. Planner analytical means the site created the comparison, score, generated opponent set or other analytical layer.

Oracle testing can also mark a narrow result oracle-validated or oracle-divergent. Untested executable behavior remains unvalidated.

Why seeded comparisons are used

If two designs are tested under the same assumptions, it is much easier to tell which change mattered. Seeded simulations make those comparisons reproducible. A modeled win rate is still an analytical estimate, not a promise about one battle in HOI4.

How Counter works

Counter starts from the two Division Lab templates and keeps the selected tech, doctrine, MIO, equipment and battlefield assumptions. It tests bounded structural changes against the same target and battle-simulates the final recommendations.

The recommendation search and ranking are planner analytical. The cards show direct equipment cost per division as current IC/div → proposed IC/div, with the absolute and percentage change. Counter does not reconstruct your live production queue or decide whether your economy can afford the recommendation.

Oracle-validated land-combat core

For HOI4 1.19.3, controlled Oracle experiments O1–O20 now support the core ordinary unarmored 1v1 resolver at the tested boundaries: a one-hour initial fire delay, the wider attack-point sampler, stochastic Defense/10 rounding with bounding, 10% defended and 40% undefended hit gates, ordinary 1–4 organization and 1–2 strength dice, and an additional 0.9 executable strength-damage scalar. These are oracle-validated only within the controlled experiments; the implementation is still an executable reconstruction.

Known boundaries

Armor/piercing special cases, armored-on-soft damage behavior, tactic execution outside the neutralized harness, broader RNG ordering and other executable-only details are not promoted by the O1–O20 result. The site does not claim bit-for-bit hoi4.exe parity.

Gauntlet

Quick Gauntlet uses roughly 500 opponents. Full Gauntlet uses 10,000 deterministic designs across eight terrain types while attacking and defending, producing 160,000 matchup checks.

Opponent generation, grades, efficiency scores and percentile rankings are planner analytical. They are useful for finding weak matchups, not for claiming that HOI4 itself assigns those grades.

Bottom line

The planner is a theorycrafting and comparison tool. It should tell you where a number came from, where the model is making an inference, and where the site is doing its own analysis. It does not claim bit-for-bit parity with hoi4.exe.
