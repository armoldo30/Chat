AIR LAB · HOI4 1.19.3

Compare aircraft without pretending the air model is exact

Build the planes you care about, hold the scenario steady and compare them. The aircraft data and the modeled air-combat behavior are labeled separately.

Build for the mission

Choose the airframe and compatible modules, then compare speed, agility, air attack, air defense, range and mission stats. The designer uses retained 1.19.3 airframe, module, slot and mission-stat data where that source coverage has been certified.

Test the exact pair you care about

Air Lab compares two built aircraft under an explicit scenario. Keep the opponent and mission assumptions fixed, change one part of your design, and run it again. That makes speed, agility, attack, defense and doctrine changes much easier to separate.

Use the result as a comparison

The current dogfight model is useful for controlled A/B testing, but its loss counts are not presented as exact hoi4.exe output. Detection, weather, night behavior, accidents, wing scheduling and other operational details are not all reconstructed at executable parity.

Why the warning matters

A model can still be useful before every internal rule is known, as long as the uncertainty is visible. Air Lab is meant to help answer “did this design improve under the same assumptions?” rather than “how many planes will I lose in the next exact in-game hour?”

Accuracy boundary

Airframe and module values are source-certified where retained. Parts of air-combat behavior remain executable inferred or unvalidated. See the methodology page for the evidence labels.
