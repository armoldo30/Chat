ACCURACY POLICY

What the planner knows, and what it does not

The data baseline is vanilla HOI4 1.19.3. Source data, executable behavior and planner-created analysis are kept separate so a useful model does not get presented as hidden game truth.

Evidence labels

game-file exact — a value or relationship retained directly from the supplied 1.19.3 files.

executable inferred — behavior supported by evidence, but not fully recoverable from the data files or narrowly confirmed by Oracle.

planner analytical — something the planner creates itself, such as generated opponents, Counter search/ranking, grades or scenario comparisons.

oracle-validated — a narrow executable behavior supported by a controlled HOI4 Oracle experiment. The label applies only to the boundary actually tested.

oracle-divergent — a controlled Oracle result that contradicts the planner mechanism being tested.

unvalidated — retained or proposed behavior that does not yet have enough evidence for a stronger claim.

Why seeded comparisons are useful

When two designs are tested under the same seeded assumptions, an A/B change is easier to interpret. That does not make a modeled win rate a promise about the next battle in HOI4. It means the planner can hold its own assumptions steady while you change the part you care about.

Counter Analysis

Counter starts from the two Division Lab templates and keeps the selected matchup context fixed. The normal search tests bounded one- and two-change candidates. If that search finds no meaningful answer and a +2 percentage-point gain is still mathematically possible, the optional deep redesign can add a bounded third change.

Final recommendations are battle-simulated. Best Raw is combat-first. Best Efficient may consider the direct IC-per-division change, modeled supply burden and edit complexity. Smallest Effective Change favors a less disruptive answer when one clears the meaningful-improvement threshold. Counter does not use saved production lines, spare factories or resource plans to accept or reject a battlefield recommendation.

Division cost

Counter reports the current and proposed equipment cost directly as IC per division, along with the absolute and percentage change. That is useful context for a recommendation, but it is not a claim that your live economy can produce the new template on schedule.

Gauntlet

Gauntlet generates deterministic opponents to look for broad strengths and weak spots. Quick mode uses roughly 500 opponents. Full mode uses 10,000 designs across eight terrain types in attack and defense roles, producing 160,000 matchup checks. The opponent generation, grades and rankings are planner analytical.

Current boundary

Some HOI4 behavior lives inside the executable and is not exposed cleanly in the game files. Tactic selection, exact RNG ordering and several other interactions are examples of areas where the planner should stay bounded rather than pretend certainty. Oracle exists to test those questions one controlled experiment at a time.

The project does not claim bit-for-bit parity with hoi4.exe, and one Oracle result never promotes the rest of the combat resolver automatically.
